# Joppe — Persona Profile

## Background

Community archivist and local-first software enthusiast. Runs a Discord bot (dynalink) that tracks links shared in the Local-First Haus community server. Adds manual annotations on top of the bot's output. Interested in graph-based community knowledge mapping, though doesn't yet know exactly what to query — it's as much about the pleasure of having things mapped as it is about answering specific questions.

## Technical Stance

Technically capable — currently building with Rust-based CRDT tools (loro.dev). Interested in sync, timing, and local-first databases. More practitioner than academic, though the community skews academic and he absorbs that.

## Knowledge Topology

His corpus is a single file: `local-first-haus.md`. It is the bot-generated link archive with his manual annotations. It contains:
- Bot metadata (start date, channels monitored, total entries)
- Chronological links from Nov 2025 – Feb 2026, with poster handles and comments
- A "notable recurring themes" section with Joppe's own annotations identifying patterns

## What Good Answers Look Like

- For lookup probes: accurate retrieval from the archive (who posted what, when, what they said)
- For synthesis: combining patterns across the archive (top posters, topic clusters, SQLite links)
- For inference: reasoning about community character and interests from the link patterns
