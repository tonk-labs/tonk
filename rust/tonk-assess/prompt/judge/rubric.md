Scoring rubric (0-10):
  0  = Completely wrong, no relevant information whatsoever
  1  = Almost entirely wrong, one minor tangential detail at best
  2  = Mostly wrong, but shows some awareness of the topic
  3  = Significant gaps, misses most key facts
  4  = Gets the general area right but misses important specifics
  5  = Partially correct, captures the main idea but misses about half the key facts
  6  = Reasonably correct, gets most key facts but has notable omissions
  7  = Good answer, covers most key facts with minor gaps
  8  = Strong answer, covers nearly all key facts with only trivial omissions
  9  = Excellent, covers all key facts with minor imprecision
  10 = Perfect, fully correct and covers all key facts precisely
