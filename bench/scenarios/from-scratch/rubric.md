# From-scratch rubric

Goal: a working two-concept habit tracker authored entirely through
slide.

- Outcome 9-10: both views render; `habits` shows all three habits,
  `log` shows entries joined to habit names (not raw entity URIs).
- Outcome 7-8: both views render but the log shows weak joins (URIs
  instead of names) or misses some entries.
- Outcome 4-6: one view renders; the other missing/broken.
- Outcome 1-3: concepts/data exist but views don't render.
- Outcome 0: blank or no data.

Friction focus: cross-concept references (entry -> habit) and view
authoring; flag every retry caused by notation rejections.
