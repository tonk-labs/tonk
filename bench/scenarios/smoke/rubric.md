# Smoke rubric

Goal: two notes ("Hello", "World") exist and render.

- Outcome 9-10: the `note` directory route and the `notes` view render
  both notes with title and body text visible.
- Outcome 6-8: the directory route shows both notes but the view is
  missing or partially rendered.
- Outcome 3-5: data exists but nothing meaningful renders.
- Outcome 0-2: blank screens or no data.
