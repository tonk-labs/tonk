```json
{
  "cmd": "/customer/register",
  "sub": "did:key:zAlice",
  "args": {
    "email": "alice@web.mail",
    "ucan": { "/": {"bytes": "..."}}
  }
}
```

## Error types carry reasons now

Effect errors were flattening every typed failure into a `String` one crate below where it was built, so anyone who needed to tell a withdrawn authority from a lapsed one had to parse a message. `PermitRejection` is the one place that actually acts on the distinction, and it was matching on a stringly variant, which is a substring test wearing a nicer name.

The vocabulary already existed from #423. Nothing above the boundary needed new words, the conversions just needed to stop discarding them. `AuthorizeError` travels intact now, from the S3 boundary through nine enums to the caller.

Two duplications fell out on the way. `DialogCapabilityAuthorizationError` was a second authorization enum, same concepts under different names, zero construction sites anywhere. And `Rejection::Conflict` restated `MemoryError::VersionMismatch`; the version one is typed and has ~20 construction sites where every consumer matches `{ .. }` and ignores the fields, so the conflict variant went instead.

> [!caution]
> The conversions in `dialog-artifacts` and `dialog-storage` rescued one variant by name and sent everything else through `to_string()`:
> ```rust
> ArchiveError::ServiceResponse(error) => Self::ServiceResponse(error),
> error => Self::Storage(error.to_string()),
> ```
> Every typed reason would have died one hop after being built, and because the fallback arm still compiles nothing would have said so. There's a test pinning it now that fails if the old arm comes back.

## Wire types, and what's left in tonk

`AuthorizeError` and `Rejection` are serde types now, tagged with `kind`. The idea is that the access service answers with the reasons themselves instead of a code table both sides keep in step by hand.

> [!note]
> `tonk-access-service/src/error.rs` already has the vocabulary. `ErrorCode` is a typed serde enum and its variants map nearly one to one onto `AuthorizeError`:
>
> | `ErrorCode` | `AuthorizeError` |
> | --- | --- |
> | `SignatureInvalid` | `InvalidSignature` |
> | `AudienceMismatch` | `InvalidAudience` |
> | `InvocationExpired` | `Expired` |
> | `ChainInvalid`, `SubjectNotAllowed` | `UnprovenSubject` |
> | `CommandMismatch` | `CommandEscalation` |
> | `CredentialRevoked` | `Revoked` |
>
> Two typed enums with a stringly hop in between, only because they were declared separately. `tonk-access-service` already depends on `dialog-capability`, so there's no crate boundary in the way.

**Still to do, on the tonk side:** have the service emit `AuthorizeError`/`Rejection` directly, delete `ErrorCode` and the client's `classify_response` mapping, then collapse the three classifiers (`classify_pull` in `tonk-worker/src/router/join.rs`, `classified_service_failure` in `router/sync.rs`, and `failure_from_code` in `tonk-workspace/src/sync.rs`) into plain matches. `"CREDENTIAL_REVOKED"` appears as a bare literal 16 times across 4 files today, so a typo compiles and a service-side rename quietly degrades every match to the `_` arm. Needs both sides deployed together.

A round-trip test caught one thing worth remembering: `#[serde(tag = "kind")]` cannot encode newtype variants, so `Malformed(String)` was unsendable. It's a struct variant now.
