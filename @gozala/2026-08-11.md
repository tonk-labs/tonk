## Accounts

I think maybe our system is bit over engineered and entangles customers with accounts. I think account is roughly equivalent of **role** in conventional access control paradigm that you grant various privileges. Then users with that have that **role** can exercise those privileges. Account on the other hand is just `did:key` identifier that aggregates delegations from various **space**'s, account can delegate aggregated capabilities (or subset of them) to the **profile** key which is basically user on some device.

Non of this requires emails or service based verification, it's just set of delegations flowing from number of spaces to an account.

## Customers

Tonk service provider does not need to care about accounts at all or treat them any different from regular spaces. Service has **customer**s and that is separate from an account _(even if very often there is 1:1 correspondence with accounts)_.

In order to acquired customers service needs to 1. obtain contact information and 2. establish billing with them. This is where we need email address from the user and (arguably their) their credit card info. Tonk can offer service to its customers under certain terms.

I think it's is important to recognize that customer can have an account, maybe even multiple of them (often times that manifests as organizations). Becoming a customer means obtaining some privileges and that can be manifested as capabilities delegated from service provider (tonk) to a principal that become a customer. I think most obvious way to do this is to delegate capabilities from the customer principal to an account.

This implies customer keys are held by the service not by the user and customer delegates limited access to the account to read customer info like usage, billing plan etc… and some write access e.g. to update contact / billing information.

This imply that customer is just another dialog repo that (tonk) service writes to and (user) account reads from. In turn this implies various dashboards could be build to provide insights into usage and access etc…
