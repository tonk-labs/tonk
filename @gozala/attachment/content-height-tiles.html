tonk-layout prototype — niri horizontal strip of columns — scroll sideways; tiles are content-height, columns scroll

Inbox

L column · single tile

A wide column with one tile. The tile fills the column's full height. The column is exactly the strip height; the strip is the viewport height. The page never scrolls vertically.

Long document

M column · content overflows the column

This tile holds more text than the column is tall. The tile itself never scrolls — it is always exactly its content height. Instead the COLUMN scrolls: scroll down within this column to reach the rest.
